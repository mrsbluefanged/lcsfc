Wave and age come from the LCSFC household study's own labels, reused from the household explorer. The study split 2020 into Wave 4 (age 13) and Wave 4a (age 14); this child export has a single 2020 round, shown as Wave 4. The 2022 round is not in the household file, so its wave and age are a provisional extension and are marked with an asterisk.

Values are national, from children's own responses. "Reason" questions let a child give more than one answer, so those shares add past 100 percent; they are marked and never stacked. Handwashing frequency, school spending and the food-insecurity score are not percentages. Sector colours follow the SitAn child-rights wheel. Boys and girls series, category grouping and sum-type labels are derived by the build tooling from the export structure, not stated by the source.
